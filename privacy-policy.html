Privacy policy

Last updated July 24, 2026

Auraluxe ("we," "us") makes hardware and software for better rest — white noise machines, sleep lamps, and the companion app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect only what helps you sleep better, and nothing more.

What we collect

  • Account information. Name, email, and password when you create an Auraluxe account.
  • Device & usage data. Sound and light settings, sleep timer and schedule preferences, routine history, and basic device diagnostics (firmware version, connection status).
  • Purchase information. Shipping address and order history. Payment card details are processed by our payment provider and never stored on our servers.
  • Support communications. Anything you share with us when you contact support.

What we don't collect

Auraluxe devices do not record audio or video in your bedroom. We don't listen, and we don't watch. Sound and light settings are stored as preferences only.

How we use it

  • To operate the app and keep your routines, schedules, and lamp settings in sync across devices.
  • To improve the product — understanding, in aggregate, which sounds and routines help people sleep.
  • To communicate with you about your order, your device, or a support request.
  • With your consent, to send occasional product news. You can opt out at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers who help us run Auraluxe (cloud hosting, payment processing, shipping) under agreements that limit their use of it, or when required by law.

Data retention

We keep account and device data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we're required to retain records for legal or accounting purposes.

Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information from the app's account settings, or by contacting us.
  • Export your routine and sleep-history data at any time.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any message we send.

Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest, and limit internal access to personal data to the people who need it to do their jobs.

Children's privacy

Auraluxe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@auraluxe.com.

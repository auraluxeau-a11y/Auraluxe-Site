Before you decide

Questions, answered plainly.

Can it run all night?

Yes. Choose continuous playback and the sound keeps going until you stop it, or set a 30, 60 or 90-minute timer if you'd rather it switch itself off. On a full charge the battery runs up to 10 hours; left plugged in, it plays indefinitely.

Can the sound and light work separately?

Yes. Use sound on its own, light on its own, or both together — they're controlled independently on the device and in the app.

Do I need the app?

No. Sound selection, volume, light colour, brightness and the timer all work from the physical controls on the device. The app adds schedules, okay-to-wake programs, child lock and saved settings.

Can I use it without Wi-Fi?

Yes — every core function works offline from the device itself. Wi-Fi is only needed for app control, schedules and voice assistants.

How long does the battery last?

The 2000 mAh battery runs up to 10 hours per charge, and the Glow plays while charging over USB-C. A full charge takes approximately 3 hours.

How loud is it?

Volume is adjustable across its range, and we recommend starting at the lowest comfortable level. We're not publishing decibel figures until acoustic output has been measured on production units at defined distances — those measurements will be published here once confirmed.

How should I use it around a baby?

Keep the Glow outside the cot, bassinet or sleep space and as far from your baby's head as practical. Start at the lowest comfortable volume, avoid maximum volume for sleep, and use the child lock to prevent accidental changes.

Can I leave it plugged in overnight?

Yes. The Glow is designed to run from USB-C power and plays while charging, so you can leave it connected. If mains power is interrupted it switches to its internal battery and keeps playing.

How does the 100-night trial work?

Your 100 nights start the day the Glow arrives. If it isn't right for you, contact our Australian support team within that window and we'll send a prepaid return label — return postage is on us — and refund the purchase price once the device is back with us. Applies to orders shipped within Australia.

What does the 2-year warranty cover?

Two years from purchase against manufacturing faults in the light, speaker, battery and electronics. It doesn't cover accidental damage or normal wear. This sits alongside your rights under Australian Consumer Law — full terms are on our terms page.

Why not just use a phone app?

The Glow keeps bedtime separate from your phone, doesn't require your phone to be beside the bed, and combines sound, light and wake-up cues in one dedicated device.

Will it help me sleep?

Auraluxe Glow isn't a medical sleep treatment. It is designed to create a consistent, calmer bedroom environment using steady background sound and adjustable light.
